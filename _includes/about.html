Hi, I'm {{ site.author.name }}, a Data Analyst based in Sydney with a Master's in Business Analytics from Macquarie University and a Bachelor's degree in Mechatronics Engineering. My engineering background shapes how I approach data: where it comes from, where it breaks, and how insights reach decisions. Building dashboards eventually led me to ask a different question: where does this data actually come from, and how does it get here reliably? That curiosity has naturally expanded my work beyond reporting and analysis into cloud data platforms, automated pipelines, and modern data engineering, to better understand the full journey from raw data to business decisions.

That direction has taken shape through two hands-on projects: a fully automated ETL pipeline (AWS Lambda, S3, Snowpipe, Snowflake) with zero manual intervention, and an end-to-end retail analytics pipeline (BigQuery, dbt, GitHub Actions CI) culminating in a Power BI dashboard and a data-driven budget reallocation recommendation. Earlier professional experience spans digital analytics, dashboard development, and sales and trade reporting across sustainability, retail, and business development environments.

My project work spans transport, energy markets, sports analytics, and retail, combining commercial analytics with modern cloud data platforms to turn raw data into decisions that get acted on. I'm also Microsoft Certified: Power BI Data Analyst Associate.
