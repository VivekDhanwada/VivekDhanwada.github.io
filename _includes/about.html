Hi, I'm {{ site.author.name }}, a Data Analyst based in Sydney with a Master's in Business Analytics from Macquarie University and a Bachelor's degree in Mechatronics Engineering. The engineering foundation shapes how I approach data: where it comes from, where it breaks, and how insights reach decisions. Building dashboards eventually led me to ask a different question: where does this data actually come from, and how does it get here reliably? That question is what's pulling me toward the infrastructure side of data work, building and automating the pipelines that feed the dashboards I used to just build on top of.

Most recently, I've been building out cloud data engineering skills through hands-on projects, most notably a fully automated ETL pipeline (AWS Lambda, S3, Snowpipe, Snowflake) that ingests and processes data with zero manual intervention. Earlier roles at Green The Chain, Unison Label, and in event and business development contexts involved dashboard development, sales and trade reporting, and pipeline analysis across sustainability, retail, and BD environments.

My project work spans transport, energy markets, sports analytics, and now cloud pipelines, using Python, SQL, Power BI, Tableau, and AWS/Snowflake to turn raw data into decisions that get acted on. I'm also Microsoft Certified: Power BI Data Analyst Associate.
